# Insights

## Facts

- The paper creates/extends functional scene graph datasets.
- It evaluates functional relationships rather than only spatial relationships.

## Paper Claims

- Functional scene graphs are useful for QA and manipulation.

## Inferences

- Human-friendly mapping cannot stop at object identity and pose.
- CAND-003 is promising but likely more annotation-heavy than CAND-001.

## Connection to Field Trends

- Shows 2025 shift from open-vocabulary category labels to affordance/function.

## Possible Contribution Angles

- Functional staleness: object is present but no longer affords the expected action.
- Instruction-conditioned functional relation query.

## What Would Change This Assessment

- If FunGraph3D is easy to use, CAND-003 becomes more feasible.
